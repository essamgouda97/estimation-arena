How the numbers are made

A submission plays each anchor in duplicate sets: every deal is played four times with the seats rotated, so luck cancels. RSA is the mean score advantage per game over the anchor. Elo is one Bradley-Terry fit over every pairwise set result, including the engine ladder ladder-v2 (200 sets), which is what places the anchors. Over budget counts card decisions slower than 0.25 s.
